# History and humanities news profile

Use this profile for news about newly published historical or archaeological research, reported excavations and finds, museum exhibitions, acquisitions, conservation, and cultural-heritage decisions.

The subject may be ancient, but the news event is current: a paper, a dig, an opening, a return of objects, or a heritage ruling.

Do not use this profile for retold anecdotes with no new publication or event, "on this day" lists, historical fiction, entertainment gossip, or invented breaking news about the distant past.
