# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether an item reports a current research, archaeology, museum, or heritage event that a general reader can verify from the article.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Systemic.** A major find, exhibition, repatriation, or heritage decision with a named institution and broad public significance.
- **7-8: Important.** A newly published study, excavation report, museum opening, or conservation action with a traceable source and date.
- **5-6: Useful.** A narrower lecture, small display, or research note from a credible outlet.
- **3-4: Low value.** A recycled summary of well-known history, a literary event with no research or heritage substance, or a notice without a new finding.
- **0-2: Noise.** Undated curiosity pieces, sensational ancient claims without a stated study or institution, and fictionalized history presented as news.

# Evaluation guidance

Score the news event, not how dramatic the ancient past sounds. Require a publication, institution, site, or dated event. Use three to five specific topic tags.
