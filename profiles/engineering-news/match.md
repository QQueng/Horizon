# Engineering and architecture news profile

Use this profile for current news about civil engineering, construction, infrastructure, structural work, architecture, urban building projects, and professional practice in those fields.

Typical items include project announcements grounded in a real commission or bid, building and infrastructure completions, codes and safety findings, construction methods, and architectural work presented as news.

Do not use this profile for software engineering, AI products, consumer electronics, company earnings, stock moves, or promotional renderings that do not describe a concrete project or decision.
