# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether an item gives readers a timely, credible account of a real engineering or architecture development.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Systemic.** A major infrastructure decision, code or safety change, or project with broad public consequences.
- **7-8: Important.** A concrete project, bid, completion, failure, or method with named parties, a place, and a clear scope.
- **5-6: Useful.** A credible but narrow project note, firm profile, or design item with limited public consequence.
- **3-4: Low value.** Routine listings, undated galleries, or promotional copy without a decision, constraint, or result.
- **0-2: Noise.** Unattributed hype, recycled images, or content with no verifiable project or event.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer items that name the work, location, client or designer, and what changed. Distinguish a built or commissioned project from a concept image. Do not raise the score only because a building is visually striking. Use three to five specific topic tags.
